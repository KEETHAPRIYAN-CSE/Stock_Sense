# StockSense Problem Statement

## The Problem
Many growing manufacturing units, retail distribution hubs, and warehouses rely on manual paper registers, static spreadsheets, or disconnected messaging channels to track raw materials and finished goods. This leads to critical operational bottlenecks:

1. **Stock Discrepancies:** Physical inventory does not match paper records due to missed logging, leading to delayed production or overselling.
2. **Lack of Traceability:** Inability to pinpoint who received, moved, or dispatched inventory, when, and under which order number.
3. **Multi-Location Confusion:** Stock moved between main warehouses, production racks, and dispatch bays is lost in transit or double-counted.
4. **Delayed Reorders:** No automated low-stock visibility or reorder threshold alerts, resulting in emergency stock-outs.

## The Solution: StockSense
StockSense is a modular, high-integrity inventory management system built around an immutable **Stock Movement Ledger**. Every transaction (receipt, transfer, delivery, adjustment) is atomically verified and recorded, guaranteeing complete auditability, real-time balance calculations, and multi-location tracking.
